Thai Overt & Mimed Speech EEG - Neural Speech Decoding Pipeline

Brain Code Camp 2026

End-to-end 32-channel, 512 Hz EEG decoding pipeline for spoken (overt) and silent (mimed) Thai speech across 80,000 trials (20 participants, 20 words).

  • Pipeline & Modeling: Implemented 5-step preprocessing (microvolt scaling, CAR, bandpass/FFT bandpower, 0.2–1.8 s windowing, normalization), trial auditing (121 flat trials), Picard ICA + ICLabel rejection, and 4-class within-subject EEGNet decoding (88.6–97.9%; ~87–90% post-ICA).
  • LOSO Audit & Calibration: Ran 10-fold LOSO across 180 word pairs (4–30 Hz Logistic Regression, 30–45 Hz shrinkage LDA), uncovering recording-group confounds (73.1% cross-half vs. 51.4% same-half; 74.4–82.9% control) and reaching 77.8% cross-mode accuracy with 20-shot calibration.

Link: github.com/nontaphatfirm/Thai-Overt-Mimed-Speech-EEG  •  medium.com/@nontaphatfirm/thai-overt-mimed-speech-eeg

AXONS Eyes Camera Health - Camera Tampering & Drift Monitoring

AXONS Corporate × Super AI Engineer Season 6

Non-blocking asynchronous sidecar monitoring CCTV analytics pipelines to flag camera tampering (FOV shifts) and scene drift before degrading downstream models.

  • Dual-Head Detection: Combined a geometric head (AKAZE features + RANSAC homography) for displacement verification with a structural head (multi-profile baselines + runtime object masking) to ignore human movement.
  • Edge Deployment: Benchmarked light-normalized residual scoring and SSIM on 3,657 samples (0.35 s edge processing) and deployed for dining-table turnover monitoring.

FormWings - On-Device Running-Form Analyzer

WellSense AIoT Hackathon, Super AI Engineer Season 6

Led software and ML design in a 10-person team building an IMU-based running-form analyzer running completely on-device.
